# Log 015: Infrastructure Pivot - GCP Quota Lockout

**Context:** 
Attempted to provision an NVIDIA L4 GPU on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) to overcome previous Out of Memory (OOM) constraints. Upgraded to a paid billing account to utilize the $300 free tier credit.

**Modifications & Rationale:**
- **Issue:** GCP automated risk systems hard-locked the Compute Engine API quota for L4 GPUs to a maximum of `0`, citing a lack of service usage/billing history.
- **Decision:** As the project operates under a strict zero-budget constraint, paying upfront to establish billing history is impossible. 
- **Action:** Abandoned GCP deployment. Pivoting immediately to Kaggle's free dual-T4 (16GB) environment. 
- **Code Changes Required:** To accommodate the smaller 16GB VRAM footprint of the T4 (compared to the L4's 24GB), I must adjust the training configurations (reducing micro-batch sizes and increasing gradient accumulation steps).

**Metrics & Environment:**
- Hardware target changed from NVIDIA L4 (24GB, Ada) to NVIDIA T4 (16GB, Turing).
- Note: T4 architecture does not support `bfloat16`. The `model_loader.py` script will automatically fall back to `float16`.
