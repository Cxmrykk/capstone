# 013 - Resolve CUDA Out of Memory on Gemma-4-E2B Forward Pass

## Context
During the `app.py train` step for the `gemma-4-e2b` model, execution halted with a `torch.OutOfMemoryError` on Google Colab's 16GB T4 GPU. The exception trace indicated the OOM occurred during the final layer `logits.float()` casting.

Gemma models feature an extraordinarily large vocabulary size of `262,144` tokens (compared to `~32,000` in older architectures). When creating the final output logits tensor `[batch_size, sequence_length, vocab_size]`, a `batch_size: 2` combined with `max_seq_length: 2048` demands roughly `4.3 GB` of VRAM instantly during the forward pass. This single allocation caused the 16GB T4 to run out of memory.

## Modifications
- **configs/gemma-4-e2b.yaml**:
  - Decreased `per_device_train_batch_size` from `2` to `1`.
  - Decreased `per_device_eval_batch_size` from `2` to `1`.
  - Increased `gradient_accumulation_steps` from `8` to `16` to maintain the effective batch size of `16`, preventing any shift in the mathematical gradient updates.
- **google-colab.ipynb**:
  - Added `os.environ["PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF"] = "expandable_segments:True"` to the environment setup cell to reduce memory fragmentation during PyTorch caching over long training sessions.
